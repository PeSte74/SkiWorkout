🏋️‍♀️ 10-Day Band & Bodyweight Training Plan

Ski & runner focused • Lower body strength • Achilles tendinitis safe • Max 30 min/day

Achilles Tendinitis Notice: This plan includes calf work on most days. Isometric holds are therapeutic. If pain exceeds 3/10 during any exercise, or lasts more than 24 hours after, reduce volume or skip that movement. Never jump on back-to-back days.
Equipment: Resistance loop band(s), sturdy chair, wall, floor space. No dumbbells required.
Upper body: Minimal — push-ups and band rows only, 2–3 sets on select days.

How to Read This Plan

  • Sets × Reps — e.g., 3 × 10 each leg means 3 sets of 10 on each side.
  • Tempo: “2-0-1” = 2 seconds down, no pause, 1 second up. “3-1-1” = 3 seconds down, 1-second pause, 1 second up.
  • Band tension: Light = single thin band · Medium = double band / thicker · Heavy = triple band or shortest loop.
  • Video links: Click the “▶ Video” button next to any exercise. It opens a YouTube search for that exact movement so you can pick a clear demonstration.
  • Day 6 is always YOGA (empty) — it floats after your hardest session (Day 5). If you feel sore after Day 5, you can move yoga to Day 7 and shift everything forward.
